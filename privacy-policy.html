Costic Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Costic ("the app") is a personal finance tracker built to work fully offline. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and what control you have over it.

Summary

  • By default, all of your financial data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly turn on Cloud Backup.
  • Costic contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. We don't sell or share your data with advertisers, because there are none integrated into the app.
  • Camera and photo access (for receipt scanning) never leave your device — text recognition runs entirely on-device.

Data stored locally on your device

Costic stores your accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, debts, dream jars (savings goals), and receipt photos in a local database on your device. This data is never transmitted anywhere unless you use one of the optional features described below. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this local data.

Optional Cloud Backup

Cloud Backup is off by default. If you choose to sign in (via a one-time email link, no password), the app will:

  • Send your email address to our backend provider, Supabase, solely to authenticate you and send the sign-in link.
  • When you tap "Back up now," or automatically once every 24 hours while signed in, upload a full copy of your financial data (the same data described above) to your private, access-controlled storage on Supabase.
  • When you tap "Restore from backup," download that data back to your device.

This data is only accessible to your signed-in account. We do not access, read, or analyze it ourselves. Supabase acts as our infrastructure provider (a data processor) for this feature only — see Supabase's privacy policy for how they handle data on our behalf.

You can sign out at any time from Settings, which stops any further backups. To request permanent deletion of a cloud backup and your account, contact us using the email address at the bottom of this page — we don't yet have a self-service deletion button in the app.

Camera and photo library (receipt scanning)

If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the app asks for camera or photo-library access to capture or select a receipt image. The photo is saved locally on your device, and the app reads text from it entirely on-device (using Google ML Kit and/or an on-device Tesseract OCR engine). No photo, and no text extracted from a photo, is ever uploaded to us or to any third party. Receipt photos are only included in a Cloud Backup if you have that optional feature turned on (see above).

Contacts (debt tracker)

If you use the "pick contact" option in the Debts feature, the app asks for one-time access to your contacts so you can select a single person. Only the name and phone number of the contact you pick are copied into that debt entry, stored locally on your device like any other Costic data. The app does not read, scan, or upload your full contact list.

Approximate location (first-launch setup only)

The first time you open Costic, it makes a single network request to a third-party IP-geolocation service (ipapi.co) to guess a sensible default currency and language for you. This reveals your device's IP address (and therefore your approximate location) to that service. Costic does not request or use precise GPS location at any point, and this lookup is not used for anything beyond pre-filling two setup fields, which you can change yourself immediately afterward.

Notifications

Costic can show local notifications for budget warnings, debt due dates, a weekly spending summary, and recurring transactions. These are scheduled entirely on your device by the operating system — no notification content is sent through a push server, and no data about your notifications leaves your device.

Data deletion

You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app, or by using the in-app "Import/Export" and account-management screens. To request deletion of any data held in Cloud Backup, contact us at the email address below.

Children's privacy

Costic is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
